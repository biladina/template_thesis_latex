# Font: Tinos

The `Tinos-*.ttf` files in this directory are the **Tinos** typeface, a
metric-compatible, open-source substitute for Times New Roman, used here to
satisfy the "Times New Roman" style requirement for `intlpaper.cls` without
depending on a proprietary font.
